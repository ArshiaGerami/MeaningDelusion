About the Book

What if meaning is not something we simply discover, but something we unknowingly participate in creating?

Human beings spend much of their lives searching for meaning. We look for purpose in our relationships, beliefs, careers, cultures, personal experiences, and even our suffering.

In Meaning Delusion, Dr. Arshia Gerami explores one of humanity's most enduring questions: Why do we need life to mean something, and where does that meaning actually come from?

This is not an argument that life is meaningless. Instead, it challenges readers to question whether meaning is something we discover or something we create through our experiences.
