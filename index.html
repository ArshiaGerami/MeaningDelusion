About the Book

Human beings spend much of their lives searching for meaning. We look for purpose in relationships, beliefs, careers, cultures, personal experiences, and even suffering.

In Meaning Delusion, Dr. Arshia Gerami explores one of humanity's most enduring questions: Why do we need life to mean something, and where does that meaning actually come from?

This is not an argument that life is meaningless. Instead, it challenges readers to examine whether meaning is discovered or created through experience.

About the Author

Dr. Arshia Gerami is a geophysicist, technology leader, researcher, translator, poet, and author based in Perth, Western Australia. Meaning Delusion explores the intersection of philosophy, human experience, and the search for purpose.
